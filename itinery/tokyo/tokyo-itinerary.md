# Tokyo Exploration

## Day 1: Shibuya & Shinjuku

- Shibuya Crossing: The world's busiest pedestrian crossing outside Hachiko exit.
- Shibuya Sky: 360-degree open-air observation deck on top of Scramble Square.
- Meiji Jingu: Shinto shrine surrounded by a tranquil 170-acre forest in Shibuya.
- Omoide Yokocho: Nostalgic narrow alleyway in Shinjuku with yakitori stalls.
- Tokyo Metropolitan Government Building: Free panoramic observatory on the 45th floor.

## Day 2: Asakusa & Akihabara

- Senso-ji Temple: Tokyo's oldest Buddhist temple in historic Asakusa.
- Nakamise Shopping Street: Traditional snacks and souvenirs leading up to Senso-ji.
- Akihabara Electric Town: Hub for retro gaming, electronics, and anime culture.
- Kanda Myojin Shrine: Ancient shrine patron of Tokyo and tech gadgets.
