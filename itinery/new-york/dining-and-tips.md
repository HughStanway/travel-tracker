# Food & Tips

## Page 1: Must-Try Eats

- Katz's Delicatessen: Legendary pastrami on rye in the Lower East Side.
- Joe's Pizza: Classic New York slice in Greenwich Village (Carmine St).
- Los Tacos No. 1: Authentic Tijuana-style tacos in Chelsea Market.
- Levain Bakery: Famous giant warm chocolate chip cookies on Upper West Side.
- Russ & Daughters: Classic Jewish bagels, lox, and babka.

## Page 2: Travel & Transit Tips

- Subway OMNY: Just tap your phone or contactless card at any subway turnstile.
- Staten Island Ferry: Free 24/7 scenic boat ride with views of the Statue of Liberty.
- Roosevelt Island Tram: Uses standard subway fare ($2.90) and offers great skyline views.
