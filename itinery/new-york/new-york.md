# Activities

## Page 1: Lower Manhatten and Financial District

- One World Trade Center: Landmark skyscraper near the 9/11 memorial.
- 9/11 Memorial: Memorial of where the twin towers were.
- The Oculus: Near the 9/11 memorial and one world trade center. Free and a monument with a good view.
- Statue of Liberty: Take the Staten Island ferry (try not to go at a busy time). Not worth paying for a tour.
- Charging Bull Statue: In the financial district.
- Wall Street: See the stock exchange. There are a few old bars around here.

## Page 2: Brooklyn Bridge, DUMBO and the Waterfront

- Brooklyn Bridge: Walk over and get some photos. Starts from downtown Manhattan.
- Manhatten Bridge: Bridge back across to Manhatten
- Manhattan Bridge: Lots of good view points.
- DUMBO: Famous viewing point of Manhattan Bridge (DUMBO Manhattan Bridge View).
- Pebble Beach: Great views of Manhattan from Brooklyn.
- Time Out Market Brooklyn: Has a free rooftop view over the city.
- Brooklyn Flea Market: Market in Brooklyn, might be nice to go if we are over there. Happens on Saturdays during the day near DUMBO.

## Page 3: Midtown Core and Landmarks

- Times Square: We can walk from hotel. Need to see it at day and night.
- Grand Central: Famous central station in New York.
- Empire State Building: One of the most famous buildings in New York.
- Fifth Avenue: Good for shopping (5th Ave).
- Louis Vuitton Building: Shaped like a massive trunk. On 5th Avenue.
- Chrysler Building: Another famous building to see.
- St. Patrick's Cathedral: New York Cathedral.
- Bryant Park: Smaller park near the Vanderbilt building.
- Radio City: Famous music hall near the Rockefeller (Radio City Music Hall).
- Broadway: Walk up the iconic street (Broadway Theatre).
- Madison Square Garden (MSG): No events booked here but nice to see it in person.

## Page 4: Chelsea, Hudson Yards and West Side

- The High Line: Walkway built over an old train line that goes along the Hudson River side of Manhattan.
- Little Island: Free viewing platform over the Hudson River.
- The Vessel: Landmark statue. Not worth paying to go up it but good to see it. Near Hudson Yards area.

## Page 5: Central Park and Upper Midtown

- Central Park: Massive - could spend a while here walking around. Can hire bikes here to ride around.
- Flatiron Building: Another famous building to see.

## Page 6: Downtown Neighborhoods

- Chinatown: Walk around, get dumplings and pork buns.
- Little Italy: Famous Italian neighbourhood.

# Page 7: East River and Islands

- Roosevelt Island Tram: Cheap tram that goes along the river up Manhattan (Roosevelt Island Tramway).
- Pier 35: View of both Brooklyn and Manhattan bridges from Manhattan side.
