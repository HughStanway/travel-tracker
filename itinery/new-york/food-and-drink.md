# New York Food & Drinss

## 1. Uptown (Upper East Side & Upper West Side)
* **Glace** (Snack / Dessert): Ice cream place known for s'mores hot chocolate. *(Location: Upper East Side)*
* **Levain Bakery** (Snack / Dessert): Highly rated bakery. Known for its cookies. *(Location: Original is Upper West Side)*

## 2. Midtown Core & Times Square
* **Rudy's Bar and Grill** (Pub / Drinks): Dive bar with $4 a pint beers in Manhattan near Times Square.
* **Jimmy's Corner** (Pub / Drinks): Historic NYC bar near Times Square.
* **Hershey's Chocolate World** (Snack / Dessert): Looks a bit like M&M world in London but might be nice to try some Hershey's chocolate.
* **Liberty Bagels** (Lunch / Dinner): Another famous bagel spot. *(Location: One near Times Square and one near Wall Street)*

## 3. Chelsea, Greenwich Village & West Village
* **Joe's Pizza** (Lunch / Dinner): Go to the one on Carmine Street as it's less busy and the original. *(Location: Greenwich Village)*
* **L'industrie Pizzeria** (Lunch / Dinner): Another highly rated pizza spot. *(Location: West Village)*
* **Mama Too's** (Lunch / Dinner): Really good New York / American Pizza (Pie's) and sandwiches. *(Location: West Village location)*
* **Magnolia Bakery** (Snack / Dessert): Famous banana pudding. *(Location: Original is West Village)*
* **Leon's Bagels** (Breakfast / Lunch): Famous bagel spot. *(Location: Prime location in Greenwich Village)*
* **Los Tacos No 1** (Lunch / Dinner): New York Tacos. *(Location: Original in Chelsea Market)*

## 4. East Village & Lower East Side (LES)
* **Katz's Deli** (Lunch / Dinner): Famous pastrami sandwich. *(Location: Lower East Side)*
* **McSorley's** (Pub / Drinks): One of the oldest saloons in NYC with cheap drinks. *(Note: This is in the East Village, it is actually quite far from Times Square!)*
* **7th Street Burger** (Lunch / Dinner): Famous burger spot. *(Location: Original in East Village)*
* **Apollo Bagels** (Lunch / Dinner): Another famous bagel spot. *(Location: East Village)*
* **Smashy** (Lunch / Dinner): $6 Smash Burgers. *(Location: Lower East Side)*

## 5. Chinatown, Tribeca & Financial District
* **Bubby's** (Breakfast / Lunch): Pancake spot. *(Location: Tribeca)*
* **Mei Lai Wah** (Lunch / Dinner): Chinatown bakery. Famous for $3 pork bun. *(Location: Chinatown)*
* **Jin Mei Dumplings** (Lunch / Dinner): Highly rated Chinatown dumplings spot and good value. *(Location: Chinatown)*
* **169 Bar** (Pub / Drinks): Bar near Chinatown. Looks like good value drinks only a few dollars per beer. *(Location: Chinatown / LES border)*

## 6. Citywide Chains & Widespread Locations
*These are major chains or local spots with so many locations around Manhattan that you can grab them in almost any zone.*
* **Chick-fil-a** (Lunch / Dinner): Chain, lots around the city.
* **Raising Canes** (Lunch / Dinner): Chain, lots around the city.
* **Olive Garden** (Lunch / Dinner): Chain, lots around the city.
* **Crumble Cookie** (Snack / Dessert): Chain, lots around the city.
* **IHOP** (Breakfast / Lunch): Famous american diner chain.
* **Lenwich** (Lunch / Dinner): Highly rated sandwich spot.

## 7. Outside Manhattan
* **The Cheesecake Factory** (Lunch / Dinner): Famous american chain, none in Manhattan but one in New Jersey.
